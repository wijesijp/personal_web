GridMusic

A grid that makes music

GridMusic is a generative music machine for Android and iOS. Movers hop from node to node across eight colored areas, following the arrows, and every node they land on plays its note — piano, marimba, saxophone, bass and drums. Tap a node to turn its arrow, and use the tools to repeat, mute, randomize or add movers: you shape the circuit, the grid makes the music.
