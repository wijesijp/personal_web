Music Technology & Creativity Education Project

January 2009 – December 2009

An interactive music application projected onto a specially constructed table, with user interactions tracked by cameras. Programmed the interactive activities and developed the graphic rendering engine for the programs running on the table. Developed for Ingenio Pte Ltd, Singapore.

BlogWall

January 2007 – September 2008

Extends SMS to a new level of self-expression and public communication by combining art and poetry, letting users project animated SMS messages and even generate multilingual poetry (English, Korean, Chinese, Japanese Haiku) from their texts. Installed at iSpace Singapore Science Center and later at Kent Ridge MRT Station. Covered by Youth.SG, ChannelNewsAsia, Freshbrainz, Saatchi Gallery, and MSearchGroove.

Mixed Reality Lab · Visual Studio 2005 C++, OpenGL, Processing, PHP

MediaME

January 2007 – July 2007

An interactive video installation composed of hundreds of videos, arranged in real time to mimic the image of a person standing in front of a blue screen — forming a mosaic that shows the bidirectional relationship between people and media. Installed at iSpace, Singapore Science Center.

Mixed Reality Lab · Visual Studio 2003 C++, OpenCV
